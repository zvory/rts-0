# Panzerfaust wind-up Set A

The selected ImageGen pass used the existing white production Panzerfaust strip as its palette and
silhouette reference. It requested exactly three equal overhead cells:

1. remove the rifle and lift the Panzerfaust directly from the back;
2. swing the launcher through a broad 45-degree shoulder transition;
3. brace and aim the launcher forward.

The soldier remains bright neutral white for the production team tint. The launcher remains warm
tan/ochre with brown straps and gray metal bands. The rifle is absent in all three cells.

The selected cells were rotated 90 degrees counterclockwise to match the production right-facing
strip. Their 160 × 160 canvases were uniformly resized to 96 × 96 (60 percent linear scale) and
centered in 160 × 112 runtime cells. At the neutral-white threshold used during review, the final
aim pose contains 1,489 whitish pixels versus a 1,501-pixel average across the three production
walking poses.
