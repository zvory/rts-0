# B7 refined team-paint building pass

Selected material-allocation refinement of the Resource Depot and Barracks. The accepted B6 Training
Center is carried forward unchanged.

- `resource_depot-whitepaint-magenta.png`: predominantly red brick, with team paint limited to civic
  stonework, tower framing, coping, and the entrance portico.
- `barracks-whitepaint-magenta.png`: all three corrugated Nissen-hut roofs are team-painted steel;
  the central front face is red brick.
- `training_centre-whitepaint-magenta.png`: unchanged accepted B6 Training Center.

Each atlas contains fixed-color, white-surface team-tint, and blurred offset silhouette-shadow
frames with transparent outer pixels.
