# Cultivator golden attacks

Generated with built-in imagegen from the live Novice, Wind/War/Magic Adept, and Master attack poses. `source/prompts.json` contains the exact prompts; `*-reference.png` files are the alignment references and `*-effects.png` files are the generated effect-only artwork on black.

Run `node scripts/art/cultivator-golden-pipeline.mjs` to rebuild, or add `--check` to verify. `source/specs.json` identifies each original character atlas, cell dimensions, and reference-frame indices. The packer converts the black effect matte to straight alpha and area-samples into four attack-effect cells (six for Novice) in the atlas's second row. The original character cells are copied byte for byte. Novice additionally appends six generated, magenta-keyed attack body poses, aligned to the original head anchor. Run the packer with `--prepare-reference` to recreate their normalized 3x2 alignment sheet.

The runtime keeps the body's normal team tint in every pose and draws the gold cells in a separate, untinted sprite. Novice uses six intermediate attack body poses with six matching gold effect frames. Magic Adept and Magic Master gather floating energy into compact hand-centered orbs; they do not draw weapon arcs. The approved Wind and War effects remain unchanged. All frames follow the existing shot envelope, facing, and lunge; Adepts and Masters retain their existing four body poses. Timing, damage, range, and gameplay rules are unchanged. Worker has no combat attack and receives no trail. Portraits and minimap body frames crop the first row using the full two-row sheet height.

Runtime sizing: Novice attack poses render at 1.59 times their authored scale, calibrated against the idle head radius. Firing-origin offsets preserve the idle head anchor; the minimap shares the correction. Magic energy renders at 2x the authored diameter, with measured source-core positions mapped to per-frame hand anchors. Body tint stays independent of effect sizing.
