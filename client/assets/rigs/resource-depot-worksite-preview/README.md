# Resource Depot worksite preview

Preview replacement for the former City Center: a fenced, open resource-processing yard with a
muddy work surface, oil silo, small foundry, crane, steel and oil stores, barrels, and a service
truck. It is a four-frame 384×384 PNG atlas: fixed-color base, team-tint mask, silhouette shadow,
and team-tinted wrench emblem.

The final emblem is the 20%-reduced review-approved wrench. It is centered, retains transparent
edge clearance, and is multiply tinted at runtime so the white fill becomes the owning team color
while the black outline remains black.

Generated sources, alpha cutouts, runtime derivatives, and provenance are retained under
`generated/` and `metadata/`.
