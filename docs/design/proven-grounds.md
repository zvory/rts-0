# Proven Grounds

## Phase 0: building brief — complete

The user explicitly requested implementation of the Proven Grounds and unrestricted combat-earned
Dao evolution. This brief records that direction before implementation. Cultivator Engineers
construct a dedicated training hall: base Disciples enter, and emerge as a chosen researched Dao
specialist. This trades oil and vulnerable training time for combat-earned insight. Opponents can
attack the building or its waiting disciples. The name is Proven Grounds, as requested.

UI description: “Send base Disciples inside for researched Dao training. Queue any mix of
Lightness, War and Magic; each costs 100 oil and takes 10 seconds. Supports a rally point.”

Art: generated from existing Cultivator Nexus, Pavilion and Tournament Grounds references.
Unique silhouette: a tall open horseshoe training gate around a small stepped exercise court,
with two unequal-height upright pillars joined by a suspended horizontal beam. Avoid the
Nexus lotus, Pavilion's three roofs, and Tournament Grounds' circular bowl. Same stone, timber,
white team-paint and isometric camera as existing faction art.

## Phase 1: rules and balance — complete

User supplied: one base Disciple per output, any mix/count of unlocked Daos, 100 oil per
training, building rally, and research-free insight evolution. The later ten-second duration
supersedes the earlier fifteen-second mention. Unspecified building numbers use a modest
prototype baseline, explicitly recorded here rather than blocking the authorized implementation.

- Cultivators only; Engineer construction, completed Portal prerequisite; 100 steel, 0 oil;
  200-tick construction, 3×3 footprint, 120 HP, armored, one-tile sight. No attack, supply,
  special repair/capture rule, or passive economy. Normal placement, collision, construction
  cancellation/refund, fog memory and damage presentation apply.
- A base Disciple with 60 insight can choose Lightness, War or Magic without any research or
  Pavilion. Existing replaceable pending choice, permanent evolution, healing, and faction gates
  remain. Pavilion Dao research instead gates paid training; researched upgrades survive building loss.
- Select the Grounds and enqueue any mixture of researched Dao training orders; one click adds
  one order, subject to the normal bounded production queue. These are plans, not extra units.
- Right-click own completed Grounds with base Disciples, or rally a Portal into it. Units wait
  outside if there is no eligible training order, insufficient oil, or another trainee is inside.
- On admission, remove one base Disciple, pay exactly 100 oil, reserve its two supply for its
  replacement, and run 300 ticks (10 seconds). No combat insight is required. Output is one
  full-health specialist with the standard evolved 60-insight invariant. Each queued order uses
  exactly one entrant; no units can be generated by queueing alone.
- Only the active trainee is inside. Queued plans cost nothing until admission. Canceling a queued
  plan costs nothing; active training cannot be canceled, avoiding loss/refund ambiguity.
- Legal exits and full rally plans use existing production rules. Blocked exits hold the finished
  trainee and reservation for retry. Destruction loses the active trainee and paid oil, releases
  reserved supply, and discards unpaid plans; waiting units remain outside.
- Owner/team/privileged views get training plans and progress; ordinary visible enemies see only
  public production progress. Durable entity state participates in checkpoints/replays.
- Lab availability and normal building encounter/targeting are included. Autonomous AI use and
  bespoke audio are deferred; existing generic building feedback applies.
