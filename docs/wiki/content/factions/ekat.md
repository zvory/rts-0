**Ekat** is a hero faction built around a single unit, {{unit:ekat}}.

## Overview

- **Start:** a {{building:zamok}} and Ekat. No resources, no builders.
- **Golems:** the Zamok trains {{unit:golem|Golems}}, which gather.
- **Ekat** has no basic attack. It fights with Dash, Line Shot and Anchor, and heals to full by consuming a Golem.
