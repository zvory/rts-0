**Kriegsia** is a combined-arms faction. Infantry come from the {{building:barracks}}, field guns from the {{building:steelworks}} and vehicles from the {{building:factory}}.

## Overview

- **Economy:** Resource Depots place Steel Mines and Pump Jacks by themselves. Engineers build and repair; they don't gather. See {{page:mechanics/economy}}.
- **Tech path:** Barracks → {{building:training_centre}} → Vehicle Works, Gun Works and {{building:engineering_complex}}.
- **Unlocks:** Tanks, Artillery, Rocket Trucks and the Scout Plane are researched at the Engineering Complex; Panzerfausts at the Training Centre.
