## Units

| Stat | Unit |
|---|---|

| Range, sight, radius | tiles (1 tile = {{TILE_SIZE}} px) |
| Speed | tiles per second |
| Cost | <span class="res steel">steel</span> <span class="res oil">oil</span> |

## Damage

- **Damage** — per hit, before armor.
- **DPS** — damage ÷ cooldown, every shot hitting.
- **Machine guns** show a range, from one bullet hitting to the whole burst. See {{page:mechanics/combat}}.
- **Damage per hit** (in *Damage dealt* / *Damage taken*) — after armor, on open ground.
- **Hits to kill (time)** — time counts from the first shot.
- **Tank targets** show front / side / rear.

## Not included

One shooter vs one target, every shot landing. Misses, splash on nearby units, research bonuses, Dao ranks and Shop items are left out.
