- Moves ×{{DAO_WIND_SPEED_MULTIPLIER}} as fast as a {{unit:scout_car}} and attacks ×{{DAO_WIND_ATTACK_RATE}} as often as a {{unit:warrior}}.

Train it at a {{building:portal}} after {{research:dao_wind}}, or evolve a Novice. At {{DISCIPLE_EVOLVED_INSIGHT_CAP}} insight it becomes a Master (see *Master rank* in the box).
