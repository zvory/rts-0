# Jeff's AI Fast-Tank Timing

_2026-07-27_

## Changes

- Jeff's AI now opens with exactly two entrenched Machine Gunners positioned close to its workers and replaces either defender after it falls below 50% health.
- Jeff's AI begins its Tank tech path immediately, produces Tanks before optional upgrades or expansion spending, and keeps that plan during defensive pressure.
- After producing two Tanks, Jeff's AI adds one Scout Car and launches the three vehicles together before AI 2.1's armored timing, using the Scout Car's longer vision.
- Methamphetamines, expansion, a second Factory, and other optional spending now wait until after the initial Tank core.
