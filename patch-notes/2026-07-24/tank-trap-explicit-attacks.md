# Tank Trap Attacks

_2026-07-24_

## Changes

- Tank Traps are no longer auto-targeted; use an explicit Attack command to clear them.
- Attacking a completed Tank Trap now queues visible completed traps within 4 tiles, with the full cluster previewed before issuing.

## Playtest watch

- Units reliably advance through clustered traps without stalling or switching to unintended targets.
